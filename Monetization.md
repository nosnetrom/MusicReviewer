# Monetization

## Recommended starting point

Add affiliate links to the existing album listening actions where streaming services offer suitable affiliate programs. These links fit a high-intent moment: a visitor has found an album and is ready to listen. Clearly disclose affiliate relationships near the links, and track outbound clicks and conversions before expanding the model.

Keep catalog browsing and source summaries open. Monetize useful next steps and additional services rather than blocking access to the core discovery experience.

## Other options

- **Reader support:** offer optional donations or memberships for visitors who value the catalog.
- **Premium features:** once accounts are available, consider saved albums, followed artists, release alerts, personal collections, and personalized discovery. Charge for convenience and personalization, not access to source summaries.
- **Sponsored curation:** offer clearly labeled sponsored artist or collection features. Keep sponsorship separate from editorial choices and notability rankings.
- **Display advertising:** consider only if traffic supports it. Intrusive ads would compete with album art and weaken the discovery experience.
- **Paid API or data licensing:** explore this only after reviewing the rights and terms for every field and asset being redistributed.

## Rights and disclosures

MusicBrainz core data is available under CC0, but this does not grant rights to every linked asset. Cover Art Archive availability does not by itself grant blanket commercial rights to album artwork; confirm the rights for each use. Wikipedia summaries require attribution and licensing information under CC BY-SA 4.0, and reuse should follow that license's terms. Obtain appropriate legal guidance before commercial redistribution.

Disclose affiliate links and sponsorships clearly and near the relevant content. Keep disclosures understandable and visible to visitors.

## Suggested rollout

1. Add affiliate listening links and a clear disclosure; measure click-through, conversions, and retention.
2. If visitors show sustained engagement, test optional reader support.
3. Build account-based premium features only when they provide recurring value, such as follows, saved collections, and release alerts.
